What a Complete SRD Contains
§ 1.1
Feature Description
Plain language readable by support with no prior context. The starting point for every incident.
§ 1.2
Customer Segments Affected
Who is impacted, their contract tier, and which SLAs apply — mapped before go-live.
§ 1.3
Observability Requirements
Specific events, metrics, and logs that must be measurable in production. Not "we'll add logging."
§ 1.4
Failure Mode Inventory
Every way it can fail — complete outage, partial failure, degraded state, data issue — defined in advance.
§ 1.5
Business Impact Pre-Classification
Severity scores loaded before go-live. Triage that took an hour now takes 30 seconds.
§ 1.6
Escalation Path Definition
Who to call at what severity. Regulatory flags. Customer communication thresholds. Decided now.
The SRD in AI & Agentic Systems
Traditional software fails loudly. AI agents fail silently — completing tasks, reporting success, and returning wrong results with no error logged and no alert fired. The SRD is the mechanism that makes these failures visible before they reach production.
Agentic AI as the Product
Six failure categories traditional software doesn't have — non-deterministic failure, silent confident failure, context window drift, tool schema drift, adversarial redirect, and mid-execution intervention triggers — each requiring its own observability requirement in the SRD.
AI-Generated Code
When AI writes the code, no one may fully understand why. The SRD becomes the specification that generated code is held accountable to — the failure mode inventory and observability requirements that exist independent of the agent that produced the implementation.
AIOps & Autonomous Incident Response
AIOps platforms execute against a framework. Supportability Engineering is that framework. Deploying autonomous incident response without an SRD means agents operating in systems with undefined failure modes and no pre-established escalation paths.
⚡
The core finding: AI agents deployed in microservices incident response fail when observability was not designed in at build time. The SRD ensures observability is designed in — before the agent is deployed, not after the first autonomous remediation goes wrong.
